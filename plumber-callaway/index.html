Slow drains, gurgling and backups in Callaway homes

A single slow sink is usually a local clog in the trap or branch line. When the tub gurgles as the toilet flushes, or two fixtures back up together, the blockage is further down in the main drain. That second pattern is where drain cleaning with a cable machine comes in.

Grease, wipes and roots are the usual culprits. If a cable clears the line but the clog returns within weeks, hydro jetting scours the pipe wall instead of punching a hole through the blockage.

Stop running the dishwasher and washer once a backup starts. Every gallon you send down a blocked main has to go somewhere, and it is often the lowest drain in the house.

Water heater leaking, cold or making noise

Water at the base of a tank is the one symptom to treat as urgent. Tanks often last about 8 to 12 years, and a rusted-through tank cannot be patched. If the tank is leaking from the top, the cause may be a fitting or the TPR valve, which is a far smaller repair.

No hot water at all points to a failed element, a tripped breaker or a dead thermostat on an electric unit, and a pilot or burner problem on gas. A water heater repair visit sorts out which one before anyone suggests replacing the tank.

Smell gas near a gas heater and the rule changes. Leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility first, then call the plumber from outside.

Before we arrive: if a pipe bursts or a leak will not stop

These four moves limit the damage in a Callaway house while the plumber drives out.

Shut off the main

Turn the house main clockwise until it stops. If the water keeps flowing, close the meter valve next to the street.

Power down wet areas

Flip breakers for the room and anything dripping above it. Leave a flashlight on rather than walking through water to the panel.

Clear the floor

Move furniture, boxes and electronics away from the spreading water. Open a drain or door if water is pooling.

Ring the 24/7 line

Report the room, the source if you can see it, and anything already shut off. The dispatcher passes that along.

Find out what your plumbing is made of

Nobody has verified a typical age for Callaway houses, so check your own. These checks take a few minutes and make the first phone call more useful.

  • Read the date label on the water heater, because it shows how close the tank is to the end of its life.
  • Look at the pipe leaving the main shutoff: copper is reddish brown, PEX is flexible plastic, gray plastic can be polybutylene, and galvanized steel looks dull gray with threaded joints.
  • Note where drains exit the house, since cast iron, PVC and clay behave differently when they clog or crack.
  • Watch the water meter with every fixture off. A moving dial means a hidden leak, which is a job for leak detection.
