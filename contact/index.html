What to tell the dispatcher

Short, specific answers help the dispatcher choose the right plumber and equipment. You do not need plumbing words, only what you can see and hear.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water or sewage is and whether you can shut it off.
  • How long it has been happening and whether it is getting worse.
  • Which fixtures are affected, such as one sink or every drain in the house.
  • Whether the building is a house, a rental or a business.
