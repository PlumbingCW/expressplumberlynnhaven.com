How to verify any Florida plumber

Plumbing contractors in Florida are licensed by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes. Each construction business must be qualified by a properly licensed individual contractor.

You can check any contractor yourself on MyFloridaLicense.com. Use Verify a License, then search by license number, individual name or business name. Do this before work begins, no matter whose name is on the truck.

Why we dispatch from Lynn Haven

Lynn Haven sits on State Road 77, the main route through the city, with North Bay along its southern edge. The Bailey Bridge carries SR 77 toward Southport, and the same highway leads toward Panama City. Dispatching from a Highway 77 address keeps those routes within reach, which matters when a call comes in at night.

Distances still depend on traffic, weather and where the nearest plumber is when you call. The dispatcher tells you what is realistic instead of quoting a promise we cannot keep.

The kinds of calls we take

Most calls fall into a handful of groups. Each has its own page so you can read about the work before you call, or while you wait for the plumber.

Water heater work covers replacement, repair, installation and tankless service. Drain and sewer work covers drain cleaning, hydro jetting, sewer line repair and camera inspection.

Leak work covers leak detection, slab leaks and burst pipes. Fixture and building work covers toilets, faucets, garbage disposals, repiping and commercial plumbing.

How we describe prices

A price depends on what the plumber finds, so this site explains what changes a price instead of printing numbers that may not fit your house. Pipe material, access, the age of the fixture and the hour of the call all play a part.

You hear the price from the plumber before work starts, and you can say no. If the job changes once a wall or floor is opened, you are told before the price changes.

After the visit

When the work is finished, the plumber explains what was found, what was done and what to watch over the next few days. Ask for that summary if it is not offered, because it helps if you later file an insurance claim or sell the house.

Keep photos of any damage you took before the plumber arrived. If a problem returns, call the same 24/7 line and describe what changed so the dispatcher can send the right person back.

What we will not do

  • Ask you to fill out a form. Everything starts with a phone call.
  • Promise an arrival time we cannot control. The dispatcher tells you what is realistic.
  • Start work before you have heard the price.
  • Claim awards, ratings or review counts we cannot show you.

Codes and standards we expect plumbers to follow

Every plumber we dispatch is expected to follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), plus the manufacturer's installation standards for the equipment being installed. Those standards cover things like drain slope, venting, relief valve piping and backflow protection.
