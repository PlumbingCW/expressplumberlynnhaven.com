Where this part of Lynn Haven sits

Lynn Haven was founded in 1911, and its original plat used numbered east-west streets with north-south avenues named for states. Ohio Avenue carries State Road 77 through the middle of it. A 2003 city redevelopment plan describes single-family neighborhoods about a block east and west of Ohio Avenue.

Monument Park sits between 8th and 9th Streets along Georgia Avenue. The Union soldier statue there dates to the early 1920s, and the city finished new paths and seating in November 2025. City Hall, built in 1928, is at 825 Ohio Avenue.

These are handy landmarks. When you call, name your numbered street and the nearest avenue, such as 6th Street and Georgia Avenue. A plumber can place that in seconds.

City line or your line?

The City of Lynn Haven runs its own water and sewer system, so the first question on any leak is whose pipe it is. The city's Public Utilities Department is at 1111 Ohio Avenue.

As a general rule, a water utility looks after the main in the street and the meter, and the homeowner looks after the pipe past the meter. Ask the city to confirm where its responsibility ends at your address, including for the sewer connection. Water bubbling up at the curb or around the meter box is a call to the city. A wet spot in the yard past the meter, or a stain on a ceiling, is a plumber's call.

Heavy rain and the sewer grid

The corner of 12th Street and Virginia Avenue, inside this numbered-street grid, was the site of an October 2021 sewage spill of roughly half a million gallons, per a Florida DEP report. After a week of heavy rain, the utility director pointed to stormwater entering the gravity sewer collection system.

That is a citywide issue, not a claim about any single house. The practical lesson is simple. If drains slow down or gurgle during a long rain, stop running water and call early. A sewer camera inspection shows whether the problem sits in your line or farther out.

Check permits before a bigger job

Permit rules vary, so ask the Lynn Haven building department at 817 Ohio Avenue what your job needs. The city says contractors email permit applications and plans as PDFs, and that processing can take up to 24 business hours.

That matters for a water heater swap or a repipe. Ask the plumber who pulls the permit, and keep the paperwork for your records. Before work starts, look up your plumber on MyFloridaLicense.com. The state license database is free to search.
