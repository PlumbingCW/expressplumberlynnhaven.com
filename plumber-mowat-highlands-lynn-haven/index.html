What Mowat Highlands is

Mowat Highlands appears on listing sites and in NeighborhoodScout, which groups it with Pine Forest Estates. Current listings place homes on Thurso Road, Inverness Court, Sutherland Road and Pentland Road, with access to Highway 390. Mowat Middle School is one of Lynn Haven's public schools.

NeighborhoodScout says most homes were built between 1970 and 1999, with some from 2000 on, and that most are single-family houses with a few mobile homes. Listing sites describe three- and four-bedroom homes of roughly 1,700 to 3,200 square feet. We found no official boundary, so this page uses those streets as anchors.

Why your own pipes matter more than the street

A neighborhood built over several decades has houses with different plumbing. Polybutylene, a gray plastic pipe, was widely installed in US homes from roughly 1978 to 1995 and is prone to failure. Galvanized steel corrodes from the inside, and PEX and copper are common today. We have no pipe survey for Mowat Highlands, so check your own house.

  1. Find your build year

    Your closing papers or the county property record give it.

  2. Look where the line enters

    Check near the main shutoff and the water heater connections.

  3. Note the color and material

    Gray flexible plastic, silver steel, copper and white or red PEX look different.

  4. Write it down

    Tell the plumber what you found when you call.

  5. Ask before you repipe

    A repiping quote should say which material goes in and why.

Everyday service calls

These apply to any single-family home and are not claims about local failure rates.

  • A slow tub or sink calls for drain cleaning, and repeated clogs may need a sewer camera inspection.
  • A rumbling tank or lukewarm showers point to water heater repair.
  • A toilet that runs or a faucet that drips is a faucet and fixture repair.
  • A wet spot that never dries needs leak detection.

HOA status varies
