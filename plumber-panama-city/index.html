Older Panama City homes and the plumbing they hide

Panama City was incorporated in 1909 and is the seat of Bay County, with about 32,939 residents in the 2020 census. Its oldest streets are in the St. Andrews historic district and in Millville, a sawmill and shipyard community from the late 1800s that was incorporated in 1913 and annexed into the city in 1926.

A house that old has usually seen several rounds of repairs. Pipe from one decade is spliced to pipe from another, so one leak often points to a stretch of failing line and not a single bad joint. Ask the plumber to inspect the neighboring run before the wall is closed back up.

If you are buying or renovating in either district, a sewer camera inspection shows the drain line condition before you commit. It takes the guessing out of old cast iron and clay.

What pipe age tells you about the likely failure

Pipe material says more about what will fail next than the street address does. The table below uses general plumbing facts, not Panama City records, so treat it as a starting point for the conversation with your plumber.

Before we arrive: burst pipe or active leak

Four steps protect a Panama City home while the plumber is on the way.

Find the main shutoff

In older houses the main valve is often at the street meter or on an outside wall. A stiff old valve may need both hands, so turn it slowly.

Kill power near water

Switch off breakers for wet rooms and ceiling fixtures. Never stand in standing water to reach a panel.

Clear the area

Lift rugs, move electronics and pull dresser contents away from the wet wall. Wet plaster in an old house can give way.

Call the 24/7 line

Describe where the water is, the age of the house and what you shut off. The dispatcher uses that to line up a plumber.

Getting across town: bridges and routes

Panama City is split by water. The Hathaway Bridge carries US 98 west toward the beach, the Dupont Bridge carries US 98 to the south, and the Bailey Bridge on SR 77 is the northern link toward Lynn Haven. The business address on Highway 77 is about 6 miles from the city by road.

Give the dispatcher your cross street and which side of the bay you are on. That detail matters more than the ZIP when a plumber is picking a route, and it saves a phone call back.

Code and permit notes for older homes

Work on older houses still follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). A job that opens a wall, such as a repipe, may need a permit, so ask the building department before work starts.
