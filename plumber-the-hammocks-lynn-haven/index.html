If you bought the house used

Many buyers inherit a house with plumbing they never saw installed. A short walk-through gives you the facts a plumber will ask for.

  1. Read the water heater label

    The tag shows the make, size and manufacture date. Tanks often last about 8 to 12 years.

  2. Find the main shutoff

    Turn it off and on once a year so it does not seize. Mark it with tape.

  3. Note the supply pipe material

    Look where the line enters the house. Copper, PEX and other plastics each look different.

  4. Check under every sink

    Look for drips, damp cabinet floors and corroded shutoff valves.

  5. Run the meter test

    With every tap off, watch the meter for a few minutes. A moving dial means a leak.

Common calls for a single-family home

These are everyday service needs, not claims about this community's failure rates.

  • A tank that leaks, rumbles or gives lukewarm water points to water heater repair or replacement.
  • A kitchen sink that backs up with the disposal running is a drain cleaning call.
  • A faucet that drips or a toilet that runs constantly needs a faucet or fixture repair.
  • A disposal that hums but will not turn is a garbage disposal repair.
