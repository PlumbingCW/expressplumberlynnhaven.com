Signs the sewer line is broken, not just clogged

A clog clears and stays clear. A broken line keeps coming back, because roots, soil or a bellied section keep refilling the same spot. If this sounds familiar, a camera visit will save you from paying for another cleaning that does not last.

  • Backups return within weeks of every cleaning.
  • Gurgling from toilets when a sink drains.
  • A sunken patch, wet spot or lush green strip in the yard above the line.
  • Sewer odor in the house or yard.
  • Cracked slab or damp drywall near a bathroom with a slab drain.
  • A cable that stops at the same distance every time.

Sewer line emergency: what to do before the plumber arrives

A collapsed line can push sewage into a tub within hours. Our dispatch line answers 24/7, so call as soon as water stops draining. Stop using every fixture, keep people and pets away from the overflow, and do not pour chemical cleaner into the line.

Before any digging, Florida law calls for contacting Sunshine 811 so buried gas, power and water lines are marked. The plumber will normally handle the call for repair work, but if you plan to dig a test hole yourself, call 811 first.

Repair options compared

Common sewer line repair methods
MethodBest forTrade-offs
Spot repairOne localized crack or root entry.Needs a dig at the defect, small repair area.
Pipe lining (CIPP)Long runs of cracked or rooted pipe with a stable shape.Cannot fix collapsed or badly bellied pipe.
Pipe burstingReplacing old pipe along its path.Needs entry and exit pits, not suited to every soil.
Open trench replacement.Collapsed, offset or very shallow pipe.Most disruption to yard, drive or slab.

Lining and bursting are called trenchless repair because they limit digging. The camera and locator decide which method the pipe will accept, and the plumber should show you the footage before you agree to anything.

How the repair decision gets made

  1. Camera the line.

    A scope runs from the cleanout toward the street and records cracks, offsets, roots and standing water.

  2. Locate the defect.

    A transmitter in the camera head lets the crew mark the surface above the problem and measure its depth.

  3. Choose the method.

    Depth, length, pipe material and what sits above the line decide between spot repair, lining or replacement.

  4. Mark utilities.

    Sunshine 811 is called before digging so other buried lines are located.

  5. Check the permit.

    Permit rules vary, so ask the Lynn Haven building department what is needed for work on your line.

After repair, a second camera pass confirms the pipe is sound. For a cleaning-only fix on a healthy line, see sewer line cleaning.

Why older Lynn Haven lines fail

Cast iron rusts and scales from the inside, clay joints let roots in, and slab-built homes often hide the line under concrete. Storms add stress. After a week of heavy rain in October 2021, the city's utility director said stormwater was entering the gravity sewer collection system, so a private lateral with open joints can take on groundwater too.

If the camera shows rooted joints or a crack, a hydro jetting pass clears the pipe before any lining so the liner bonds to clean walls.

What affects the price of sewer line repair

  • Length of pipe to be repaired and its depth.
  • Spot repair versus lining versus full replacement.
  • What sits above the pipe: lawn, driveway, slab or a tree.
  • Permit and inspection requirements.
  • Restoration of the dug area.
  • After-hours dispatch

Insurance may cover a sudden, accidental line break but commonly excludes wear, root damage and slow leakage. Read your policy or ask about a sewer line endorsement. For your real number, call the 24/7 line after a camera visit.

Repair methods and standards

Cured-in-place lining follows ASTM F1216, a published standard for that method. Whether lining is allowed or a trench is needed depends on the pipe condition, and local permit rules vary, so the plumber confirms the approach with the Lynn Haven building department.
