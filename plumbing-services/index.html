Emergency work and planned work are handled differently

Emergency calls are those where damage grows by the minute, such as an active leak or sewage on the floor. Those go to the front of the line, around the clock.

Planned work covers fixture upgrades, water heater replacement and new installations. Those are scheduled, and the dispatcher offers the next opening. Tell them if you have a deadline, such as a house sale or a family visit.

Codes that apply across every service

Whatever the service, the work falls under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). That code sets rules for drain slope, venting, water pressure limits and backflow protection, and local permit rules vary, so the plumber confirms what your job needs.
