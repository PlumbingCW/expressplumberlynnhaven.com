What Northshore means on the map

North Bay hugs the north and west sides of Lynn Haven. Northshore Road, Northshore Circle, North Bay Drive and Beachcomber Drive run toward the water on the city's north side, and listing sites label some of these homes as North Shore or Northshore. Custom homes near North Bay often have water views and private docks, according to Homes.com's local guide.

We found no official neighborhood boundary, so this page works from those streets. If you live nearby and the streets sound familiar, the advice below applies.

Bigger lots change where leaks hide

County records for ten homes on Northshore Circle show lots from about a third of an acre to more than an acre, with a median near 0.8 acre. That is one street, not the whole area, but it points to a practical issue. A supply line or sprinkler feed on a large lot can leak a long way from the house.

  • Watch the water meter with every tap and sprinkler off. A moving dial means water is escaping.
  • Look for green patches or soft ground in the yard on dry days.
  • Listen at the main shutoff for a hiss when everything is closed.
  • Note any rise in the water bill and tell the plumber the month it began.

A leak detection visit uses listening gear and pressure tests to trace a leak without digging up the lawn.

Sewer or septic: know which you have

Whether a lot is on city sewer or its own septic tank changes the repair. We could not confirm which applies on each Northshore street, so check your own property. Your closing papers, the city's utility office or the tank lid in the yard will tell you.

On sewer, a repeated backup points to a clogged line, and sewer line cleaning clears it. On septic, a backup can mean a full tank or a failing drain field, and that is a different service call. Tell the person who answers which one you have. Whoever you hire, check the contractor at MyFloridaLicense.com. Plumbing work follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing.

Salt air and outdoor fittings

Homes near saltwater see more corrosion on outdoor hose bibs, exposed valves and irrigation parts. This is a general coastal issue, not a local measurement. Check outdoor fittings each spring for green crust or a drip at the stem.

A leaking faucet or hose bib is cheap to fix now and can hide a bigger leak later.

Across the bay
