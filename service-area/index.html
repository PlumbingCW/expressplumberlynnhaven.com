Lynn Haven itself

Lynn Haven runs its own water system. The city's 2024 annual water quality report says five deep wells in the Floridan Aquifer serve most customers, with purchased surface water from Bay County Utilities serving the rest. The report does not list hardness, so test your own tap before you buy a softener.

Home ZIP code 32444 is our base. If you are in Lynn Haven, you are as close as the dispatcher can send a plumber, and the pages for water heater replacement and drain cleaning cover the work we are called for most.

What to have ready when you call

Give the dispatcher your street address, not only a ZIP code, because ZIP 32404 covers both Callaway and Parker and 32401 reaches Panama City and Springfield. A cross street helps in neighborhoods where several roads share a name.

Tell the dispatcher what you can see: where the water is, whether you can shut it off and how long it has been going on. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility before you call a plumber.

How dispatch decides who goes first

When several calls arrive at once, active leaks, sewage backups and failed water heaters go ahead of routine work. The dispatcher asks where the water is, whether it can be shut off and whether anyone is at risk, and the answers set the order.

A planned job such as a fixture upgrade is booked for the next available appointment. If you have a deadline, such as a house closing, say so on the call. Distance from Lynn Haven is one factor among several, and the dispatcher tells you what is realistic for your address instead of promising an arrival time.
